ABOUT NOVA

Built for the
next move.

Nova is a concept e-commerce brand built around a simple idea: make digital shopping feel as considered as the products themselves.

OUR MISSION

Good design should disappear into the experience.

Every interaction is intentional — from the first scroll to the final checkout. Nova pairs editorial art direction with a fast, responsive shopping flow.

This project demonstrates front-end development skills including responsive CSS, reusable JavaScript components, URL-based product pages, filtering, sorting, localStorage cart state, wishlist interactions and polished micro-animations.
